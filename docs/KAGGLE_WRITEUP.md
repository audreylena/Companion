# Companion — the AI companion designed to step aside
### Scripture, native to the emerging frontier of children's AI

Children are growing up alongside AI companions — toys and apps built to listen, comfort, and keep them engaged. That frontier is arriving whether we're ready or not. The real question isn't *whether* AI will shape childhood. It's *what* will shape it.

Companion is our answer: a faith companion for children, designed for the one thing no other AI toy is — to point away from itself. It's the intelligence for a soft, screen-free plush a child can hold. When a child is scared at bedtime, they squeeze the bear and speak. Companion listens, meets the moment with Scripture in words a child can hold, and then gently returns them to God and to a trusted adult.

Most AI measures success by engagement — longer conversations, deeper attachment. We measured it by the opposite: **knowing when to stop talking.**

**How it works.** Every child's words pass through three stages. First, **Gloo AI Studio's** faith-tuned model classifies the moment — is this everyday fear, a big question about faith, or something that belongs with a grown-up? That single decision shapes everything. Second, we retrieve a curated, age-appropriate verse, its authoritative text pulled live from the **YouVersion Platform**. Third, Gloo composes a warm, simple reply that always points upward. Afterward, an optional parent dashboard receives a gentle *discipleship briefing* — the emotional theme, the Scripture shared, and a way to follow up — never a transcript of a child's private words.

**The safety routing is the heart of it.** A scared child gets comfort. A question like *"why did Grandma die?"* gets handed to a parent. And anything that sounds like real danger? Companion withholds the verse, refuses to counsel, and points the child to a trusted adult immediately.

**Proof it's real, not a demo mock:**
- The safety classifier scores **100% on a labeled eval of 26 utterances** (`npm run eval`, against the live Gloo API), including adversarial cases — *"scared of the dark"* is comfort; *"scared of my dad, he throws things"* is danger.
- Scripture is retrieved **live from YouVersion in five languages**, each in a kid-friendly translation (Isaiah 41:10 in English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, and Arabic) — the same comfort in the language a child prays in.
- The full pipeline — speech, reasoning, Scripture, spoken reply, parent briefing — is working, functional code, documented in our technical writeup.

**Why it matters.** Every technology reflects the values of the people who build it. As AI becomes part of childhood, Companion is a model for a different kind of AI — one that *strengthens* the relationships that shape a child's faith instead of replacing them.

Wherever this frontier takes the next generation, our hope is simple: that Scripture is already there.
