# Companion — the AI companion designed to step aside
### Scripture, native to the emerging frontier of children's AI

Children are growing up alongside AI companions — toys and apps built to listen, comfort, and keep them engaged. That frontier is arriving whether we're ready or not. The real question isn't *whether* AI will shape childhood. It's *what* will shape it.

Companion is our answer: a faith companion for children, designed for the one thing no other AI toy is — to point away from itself. It's the intelligence for a soft, screen-free plush a child can hold. When a child is scared at bedtime, they squeeze the bear and speak. Companion listens, meets the moment with Scripture in words a child can hold, **reads it aloud**, and gently returns them to God and to a trusted adult.

Most AI measures success by engagement. We measured it by the opposite: **knowing when to stop talking.**

**How it works.** Every child's words pass through three stages. **Gloo AI Studio's** faith-tuned model first classifies the moment — everyday fear, a big question about faith, or something that belongs with a grown-up? That decision shapes everything. Then we retrieve a curated, age-appropriate verse, its text pulled live from the **YouVersion Platform**. Then Gloo composes a warm reply that points upward. Afterward, an optional parent dashboard receives a gentle *discipleship briefing* — the emotional theme, the verse shared, and a way to follow up — never a transcript of private words.

**The safety routing is the heart of it.** A scared child gets comfort. *"Why did Grandma die?"* gets handed to a parent. Anything that sounds like real danger? Companion **withholds the verse**, refuses to counsel, and points the child to a trusted adult immediately.

**Under the hood:**
- The safety classifier scores **100% on a labeled eval of 26 utterances** (`npm run eval`, live Gloo), including adversarial cases — *"scared of the dark"* is comfort; *"scared of my dad, he throws things"* is danger.
- Scripture is retrieved **live from YouVersion in five languages**, each in a kid-friendly translation — the same comfort in the language a child prays in.
- The full pipeline — speech, reasoning, Scripture, spoken voice, parent briefing — is working code.

**What's next.** The plush itself — soft hardware with a mic, a speaker, and a gentle glow, so a child never touches a screen. Real-time family persistence, short curated Bible *stories* per feeling, more heart-languages, and connection to a family's own church — always *strengthening* the relationships that shape a child's faith, never replacing them.

**Why it matters.** Every technology reflects the values of its makers. As AI enters childhood, Companion is a model for a different kind — one that leads a child toward God and the people who love them.

Wherever this frontier takes the next generation, our hope is simple: that Scripture is already there.
