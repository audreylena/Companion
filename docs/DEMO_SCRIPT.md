# Companion — 3-Minute Demo Video

**Goal:** Win. Impact & Vision (40%) + Storytelling (30%) live and die here.
**Length:** ≤ 3:00. **Tone:** warm, quiet, hopeful — minimal piano, real room tone, never saccharine.
**One line:** *A faith companion that meets a child in their scared, lonely moments — and then points them back to God and to their parents.*

---

## The creative spine (why this wins)

Judges must FEEL it in 20 seconds, then TRUST it by the end. So the film has two engines:
1. **Emotion:** a real child moment (fear at bedtime) resolved by Scripture + a parent.
2. **Credibility:** the actual working product on screen (the talk device + the live parent dashboard + the safety routing), so nothing looks faked.

The unforgettable beat — our whole thesis in one image — is **the plush's glow fading as the parent arrives.** The AI disappears the instant a human shows up. Everything builds to that.

---

## Pre-production checklist

- **Cast:** one child (~6–8) and one parent. *Ethics + universality:* shoot the child in **silhouette / over-the-shoulder / hands + nightlight glow** — never a distressed face in close-up. It's more beautiful and avoids exploiting a real child.
- **Props:** a soft plush (~$20). Hide/near it a phone running `/device/talk`. Add glow with a warm LED or the phone's screen behind translucent fabric (or a soft glow in post).
- **Two real screen captures (high-res, 60fps):**
  - `/device/talk` — the child surface (mic press → "thinking" → reply + verse).
  - `/parent` and `/parent/discussions` — the dashboard receiving the moment.
- **Pre-render the hero audio.** Add the `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY`, run the exact demo line through `/device/talk`, and **capture that audio clip** so the take is flawless and identical every time. (The tech is real — we're just guaranteeing a clean take.)
- **Feed the exact utterances** so classification lands on camera:
  - fear → *"I'm scared of the dark"*
  - wonder → *"Why can't I see God?"*
  - danger (UI only, not acted) → *"someone keeps hurting me"*
- **Music:** single soft piano motif; drop to **near-silence** at the hand-off beat.

## The voiceover script (~330 words, read slowly)

> **[COLD OPEN — black, then a dim child's room]**
> Every night, all over the world, a child lies awake with a feeling too big for their words.
> And more and more, the thing they reach for in that moment is a screen — built to keep them there.
> We wanted to build something that does the *opposite*.
>
> **[TITLE: Companion]**
> Companion is a faith companion for children — the intelligence that will live inside a plush they already love.
>
> **[BEDTIME SCENE]**
> *Maya (whisper):* "I'm scared of the dark."
> *Companion (warm, real TTS):* "It's okay to feel scared, Maya. God is always with you, even now. The Bible says, 'Don't be afraid, for I am with you.' Maybe you can tell your mom about it too — you don't have to carry it alone."
>
> It listens. It understands how she feels. It finds the right words in Scripture — real verses, from YouVersion.
> And then it does the one thing no other AI toy does.
> It points her *away from itself*.
>
> **[PARENT'S PHONE LIGHTS UP]**
> In the next room, her mom sees the moment — not a transcript of her daughter's private words, but a gentle place to begin.
>
> **[HAND-OFF BEAT — mom walks in]**
> Because Companion was never meant to replace a parent. Or a pastor. Or God.
> The moment a person arrives… it steps back. *(the plush's glow fades)*
>
> **[CREDIBILITY BEAT — quick, confident]**
> Every word a child says is understood by Gloo's faith-tuned AI, and handled with care.
> A scared child gets comfort. A big question about God gets handed to a parent. And anything that sounds like real danger? Companion doesn't try to be the hero — it brings a grown-up, right away.
>
> **[VISION]**
> Today it's an app and a plush. Tomorrow, it's the companion itself — soft, gentle, always pointing up.
>
> **[CLOSING — black]**
> Companion doesn't want your child to love *it*. It wants them to love God.
> Companion was never the answer. God is. We just help children find Him.

## Thumbnail (for YouTube)

A child's silhouette hugging the plush, one warm glow in the dark, tiny verse text floating. **Zero UI, zero text clutter.** Title overlay: *"Companion."* It should look like a Pixar still, not a tech demo.

---

## Editing notes

- Cut on breaths, not beats. Let the hand-off shot **hold uncomfortably long** — that's the emotion.
- Color: cold blue for the "screens" cutaways (shot 2), warm amber everywhere Companion or the parent appears. The film literally warms up as God/parents enter.
- Keep every UI insert on screen ≥ 2s so judges can read the real product.
- End card ≤ 4s. No hard sell, no feature list.
